# Candidate Guide

Welcome to the QuizCraft Candidate Portal. This guide explains how to navigate your dashboard, take tests, and review your performance.

## 1. Dashboard
After logging in, you will see your **Candidate Dashboard**. This includes:
- **Available Tests**: Exams currently active and assigned to you.
- **Completed Tests**: Exams you have already submitted.
- **Practice Center**: Access to AI-generated mock tests based on your weak areas.
- **Bookmarks**: Questions you saved for later review.

## 2. Taking a Test (CBT Workspace)
When you start a test, you enter the secure **CBT Workspace**:
- **Timer**: A live countdown clock is displayed at the top. If the time expires, your test is auto-submitted.
- **Navigation Grid**: Use the question grid to jump between questions.
- **Status Indicators**: Questions are color-coded (Answered, Unanswered, Marked for Review).
- **Proctoring**: If enabled by the admin, your webcam and tab activity may be monitored. Ensure you remain in the test tab.

## 3. Reviewing Results
Once a test is submitted (and if the admin allows immediate result viewing):
- **Score Breakdown**: View your total score, accuracy percentage, and time taken.
- **Question Review**: See the correct answers alongside your chosen options, complete with detailed explanations.

## 4. Practice Center
Use the **Practice Center** to improve your skills:
- The system automatically detects your weak topics based on past tests.
- Click **Start Practice** to get a custom, AI-curated quiz targeting those specific areas.

## 5. Resume Capability
If your browser crashes or you lose internet connectivity:
- Simply log back in.
- Click **Resume Test**.
- The system periodically syncs your answers to the server, so you will pick up exactly where you left off.
